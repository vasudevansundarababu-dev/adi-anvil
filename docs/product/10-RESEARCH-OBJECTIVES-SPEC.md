# F01 — Research Objectives

Version: 0.8 functional draft
Updated: 27 September 2026  
Status: F01 foundation confirmed; first SKU clarified as 200 multi-domain business cases with partial Bodhium supply and lab-specific ADI enhancement; detailed workflow proposed

## 1. Purpose

Turn a research need into a versioned brief that states what capability ADI will investigate or support, which evidence matters, what assets are in scope, and how the work will be accepted. The brief guides task/environment production, evaluation, learning-asset preparation, reporting and delivery without requiring an ADI model or training service.

The user approved both objective origins and collaborative authoring, with **ADI Researchers, Advisors, ADI Sales and selected customer researchers** (F-D08). Advisors supplement rather than replace ADI Researchers. The user then selected scope-approval option B: **ADI Researchers and Advisors recommend; an ADI Product Owner gives final objective-scope approval** (F-D09). Day-to-day brief ownership, detailed permissions and downstream execution authority remain separate; other proposed fields, states and rules below still require review.

## 2. Existing product boundaries

- Frontier serves capability diagnostics, learning-data/environment delivery and controlled improvement studies. The lab owns model optimization.
- A diagnostic objective does not require Training Packs. No model optimizer, learned TTM, Enterprise deployment or business-monitoring dependency is added.
- F01 describes the objective and acceptance basis. Praxis owns commissioned SME authoring/business-content review; F03 qualifies accepted or approved-partner tasks for research and owns capability/family mappings and enhancement lineage. Data Preparation handles supporting records; Environment Studio supplies controlled worlds when needed; Evaluate owns the executable assessment protocol.
- A benchmark mapping is a proposed measurement relationship, not proof that an asset improves that benchmark.
- Scope agreement, scientific approval, spending authorization, delivery approval and customer acceptance are distinct. An accepted objective cannot launch paid work automatically.
- Customer or third-party confidential material requires permitted use and audience controls; an uploaded brief is source material, not authority to execute instructions embedded in it.

### 3.1 Confirmed authoring direction — four collaborating groups

The user's correction (F-D08) supersedes the earlier replacement statement in F-D07. F01 supports **ADI Researchers, Advisors, ADI Sales and selected customer researchers** collaborating within authorized objectives/projects. Do not require an Advisor to be an ADI employee, and do not grant every customer researcher access automatically. This is an eligible participant list, not a requirement that every objective involve all four groups.

Working responsibility breakdown for detailed review:

| Participant | F01 contribution | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ADI Researchers | Draft/refine the technical objective, assess available evidence and propose measurement and feasibility requirements | Being a participant does not settle objective ownership or grant new execution/approval rights |
| Advisors / assigned Advisor group | Contribute specialist insight, challenge assumptions and help shape/review the brief | Not replacements for ADI Researchers; no automatic sole ownership, run, budget or release authority |
| ADI Sales | Capture customer needs, coordinate contributors and record delivery constraints | Cannot invent evidence, promise unsupported results or approve scientific findings by virtue of sales access |
| Selected customer researchers | Contribute customer objectives, constraints, permitted evidence and feedback within the authorized project | Explicitly selected/invited access; no cross-customer/private ADI access or automatic spending/customer-acceptance authority |

Support multiple contributors without assuming that every Advisor, ADI researcher, salesperson or customer sees every objective. These are permission-scoped roles, not blanket access based on title. Participation does not automatically confer task-SME, scientific-assessment or Admin approval rights. The responsibility breakdown above is a proposal; the four eligible groups are confirmed.

Detailed invitation, edit/comment permissions, assignment, confidential evidence access and offboarding mechanics are proposed in section 9, with implementation specifics still open. Customer-requested objectives may still be entered by ADI; invited collaboration is supported but customer login is not mandatory for every brief. ADI-initiated work does not require an invented customer.

No sole technical owner has been selected. Evaluation execution, learning-asset engineering, domain review and customer-side training roles are unchanged unless separately agreed. No user accounts or invitations are created by this specification.

## 4. Proposed brief content

1. Objective identity/version, origin, assigned contributors and roles, assigned ADI Product Owner for scope approval, technical author/coordination owner once decided and intended audience; customer reference only where applicable.
2. Research question: capability and observable behavior, with examples and explicit exclusions.
3. Target system: model plus agent runtime/tools where known, or a clearly labeled target-to-be-confirmed.
4. Evidence available: observed failures, customer reports, public results or an untested hypothesis; baseline known/pending/not applicable with a reason.
5. Intended offer: diagnosis, learning assets/environment or an intervention study; not every objective needs all three.
6. Proposed measurement: primary outcome, guardrails, relevant suite/benchmark, comparator and independent validation needs. Evaluate owns the detailed executable protocol.
7. Delivery scope: asset types, meaningful coverage/families, customer interface, permitted data/execution locations, rights and exclusions.
8. Acceptance basis: evidence/quality/compatibility checks, acceptance owner and unresolved thresholds. Separate delivery acceptance from a scientific hypothesis of improvement.
9. Proposed effort/budget/timing and dependencies, visibly distinguished from authorized spend or a promised delivery date.
10. Review history, unresolved assumptions, version changes and downstream references.

These are proposed logical fields, not a final database schema. Section 8 defines proposed stage requirements. Do not require private customer details for an internal research objective or invent missing evidence.

## 8. Stage requirements — proposed

Every objective has an organization/project boundary, stable identity, immutable submitted revisions and an audit history. Origin and research starting point are independent fields. Selecting a benchmark never authorizes importing protected benchmark cases.

| Stage | Required information | Allowed unknowns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Save draft | Title, origin, starting point, question, creator and project access | Baseline, target system, benchmark mapping, staffing and quantities can be Pending |
| Request scope review | Observable capability/behavior; evidence labeled observed, customer-reported or hypothesis; offer; in/out scope; proposed measurement and acceptance basis; rights constraints; assigned coordinator, required recommenders and Product Owner | Actual baseline results and nonessential implementation details can remain pending with owner and blocking stage |
| Approve scope | Recommendations on the exact revision; material objections resolved or explicitly returned for changes; Product Owner decision; work boundaries and unresolved dependencies identified | Approval can permit specification/design while execution remains blocked |
| Dispatch production/enhancement brief | Approved objective revision; capability/coverage requirements; permitted source use; case gaps and expected task/reference/rubric outputs; named production owner; Praxis PM/project mapping if using Praxis; explicit authorized dispatch | Task counts/timing remain estimates unless separately committed; staffing completion is not assumed |
| Request evaluation readiness | Frozen measurement plan; target model/harness; task/environment/grader versions; sample/split policy; metrics and exclusions; evidence-sharing boundary; assigned evaluation owner | Missing execution-critical input blocks readiness, not draft editing |
| Launch evaluation | F06 protocol approval plus applicable distinct-Admin authorization and explicit launch | No missing rights, scope, resource limits or required runtime qualification |

A baseline may be the output of the first authorized diagnostic rather than an entry prerequisite. A study comparing improvement must identify the comparator and intervention before outcome inspection. A capability-led diagnostic need not invent an external benchmark. Any external benchmark claim needs an approved benchmark-specific protocol and permitted evidence.

Counts must distinguish independent families, task instances, generated variants, source versions, repetitions and valid scored episodes. A quantity target is not a scientific sample-size justification.

## 9. Roles and access — proposed detailed policy

Eligible collaborator groups and Product Owner scope approval are confirmed. The following mechanics are F-A01/F-A02 proposals:

| Role | May do within assigned project | May not do from this role alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ADI Researcher coordinator | Maintain brief, request reviews, propose capability/measurement, prepare production handoff | Approve their own final scope as Product Owner or bypass separate execution controls |
| Assigned Advisor | Edit permitted technical sections, attach permitted evidence, recommend or request changes | Access all ADI/customer projects, hidden tests or confidential traces by default |
| ADI Sales | Draft discovery/customer constraints, comment and request commercial clarifications | Change frozen measurements, scientific verdicts or approve result disclosure |
| Selected customer researcher | Edit/comment on explicitly shared brief sections and contribute authorized evidence | View private ADI work, other customers, reference solutions or contributor records automatically |
| Assigned Product Owner | Approve, request changes or decline the reviewed scope revision | Authorize spending, source disclosure, scientific conclusions or customer acceptance by scope approval alone |
| Admin | Manage scoped invitations and access, revoke access with audit | Impersonate a scientific recommender or scope approver by administrative privilege alone |

Proposed minimum review: one assigned ADI Researcher recommendation and one assigned Advisor recommendation, then a distinct Product Owner scope decision. Extra optional contributors need not approve. The required reviewer list is fixed for the submitted revision. Missing required review keeps it pending; changing reviewers invalidates the affected review requirement and is audited. Technical objections return for resolution; Product Owner approval is not a mechanism to relabel an unresolved validity problem as scientifically solved.

This is not a confirmed staffing/quorum choice. If the team cannot staff it, select a documented alternative before implementing the workflow; do not bypass the gate automatically. Customer input is not customer contractual acceptance. Revocation removes future access, and historical comments remain attributed under the retention policy without promising recall of downloaded material.

## 10. States, revision changes and errors — proposed

Objective revision: Draft → In review → Scope approved. Review may produce Changes requested or Declined. An approved revision is immutable; changes create a successor draft. Superseded, Cancelled and Archived preserve history.

Execution and production progress are linked work records, not new interpretations of Scope approved. A dashboard may summarize Not requested / Requested / In progress / Blocked / Completed from downstream records without owning their transitions.

- Submission pins the content digest, required reviewer assignments and expected revision. Stale edits/recommendations are rejected with a visible conflict and a comparison view.
- Changes to question, target capability, evidence interpretation, scope, measurement, rights, recipient, counts committed to a contract or acceptance basis require a successor revision and relevant reapproval. Typographic commentary can be appended but cannot rewrite approved content.
- A successor does not silently update an active run or commissioned Praxis project. Show the affected dependency list and require each work owner to accept a change request. Cancellation requests do not claim that in-flight services have stopped.
- A production dispatch is explicit and idempotent. If delivery is uncertain, retain Pending confirmation; do not create a second project/request merely because the client retried.
- A missing Praxis connection or project reference permits brief drafting but blocks the applicable automated dispatch. If a manual PM handoff is separately authorized, record its recipient, brief version, time and eventual project mapping.
- A customer-reported failure stays reported until reproduced; an unobserved suspected failure stays a hypothesis. New evidence does not retroactively change the evidence label in an old report.
- Unavailable models, denied access, unapproved source rights, incompatible benchmarks and insufficient measurement design produce named blockers, never fictional defaults or a fabricated readiness badge.

No approved/completed event starts paid work, publishes a report or delivers data automatically.

## 11. Screens, logical contracts and handoffs — proposed

Screens: Objective List; Brief Editor; Capability and Measurement; Evidence and Rights; Collaborators and Review; Version Comparison; Production Handoff; Linked Work and Readiness. Drafts display Pending explicitly. Audience visibility is enforced server-side, not only by hiding a tab.

Logical records:

- `ResearchObjective`: stable ID, origin, starting point, current draft/approved revision references, project and lifecycle.
- `ObjectiveRevision`: question, capability references, scope, offer, intended target, measurement proposal, deliverables, rights/visibility, quantities with units, open dependencies and digest.
- `ObjectiveRecommendation`: recommender, required role, exact revision/digest, recommendation, reasoning summary, objections and time. This is a human review rationale, not a model's hidden reasoning.
- `ObjectiveScopeDecision`: assigned Product Owner, exact reviewed revision, outcome, rationale, time and unresolved non-execution dependencies.
- `ProductionRequest`: objective/capability/coverage references, Praxis project/template mapping, expected accepted outputs, status and change requests. Payload and transport follow the integration specification.
- `ReadinessReference`: owning downstream module, artifact/version, declared state, evidence reference and blocker. F01 cannot modify the downstream owner's approved state.

Service operations to specify in the implementation stack: create/save draft, submit revision, recommend/request changes, approve/decline, create successor, prepare/dispatch handoff, cancel/archive, query dependencies. Each requires authorized scope, version/concurrency checks, audit and idempotency where applicable. These are logical commands, not claims of implemented HTTP routes.

Handoffs: F03 qualifies partner/Praxis source records, records enhancement needs and prepares commissioned production requests where required; F02 supplies permitted supporting records/ontologies; F04 binds tasks to environments when needed; F06 owns the executable measurement protocol; F07 governs suite/benchmark registration; F05 prepares optional learning assets; F08 produces evidence-linked reports; F09 owns generic pack assembly/release. A diagnostic can follow F01 → F03 → F06 → F08/F09 without F05 or an interactive simulator; add F04 for the required controlled execution/environment contract.

## 12. Proposed F01 acceptance tests

- F-OBJ01: An ADI-originated draft saves without a customer, and a customer-requested draft retains its permitted source attribution.
- F-OBJ02: Either benchmark-led or capability/failure-led entry is supported; a named benchmark does not imply permission to import its data.
- F-OBJ03: A hypothesis, customer-reported observation and independently measured failure remain visibly distinct.
- F-OBJ04: Only explicitly assigned collaborators can read/edit permitted brief sections; customer/advisor access does not extend to hidden tests or unrelated projects.
- F-OBJ05: Scope approval uses the assigned Product Owner and required recommendations on the same immutable revision under the configured review policy.
- F-OBJ06: A stale edit/recommendation cannot approve an altered brief; a successor requires affected reviews again.
- F-OBJ07: Missing baseline results may allow scope design, but missing measurement/execution prerequisites block evaluation launch.
- F-OBJ08: Scope approval triggers neither a provider call nor paid production work, dispatch, publication or delivery.
- F-OBJ09: A retried production handoff cannot create a duplicate request; uncertain acknowledgement stays explicit.
- F-OBJ10: A changed/cancelled objective does not silently mutate an in-flight Praxis project, environment or evaluation protocol.
- F-OBJ11: Diagnostic-only delivery can bypass F05; no ADI model, learned TTM or optimizer is required.
- F-OBJ12: Quantities distinguish families/instances/variants/attempts, and untested uplift is never used as completed acceptance evidence.

These tests are specifications, not executed test results. The 200-case business-diagnostics SKU is confirmed; named owners, lab capability profile, quality thresholds and implementation schemas remain to be agreed.
