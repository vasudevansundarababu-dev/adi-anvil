Version: 0.2 · Review draft · 27 September 2026
Modules: F07 Benchmarks; F08 Reports

## 1. Benchmark registry

Maintain one versioned registry for research suites/protocols, source licenses, intended measurements, allowed adaptations, task/split identities, grading requirements, target/harness conditions and baseline evidence. Administration supplies connection/access setup; Evaluate executes pinned protocols.

GDPval remains a requested candidate external benchmark integration, not a completed adapter or universal measure of interactive long-horizon behavior. Validate current source/version/access/license/protocol before implementing or claiming comparable results. Additional registered benchmarks require the same controls.

**SKU 01 is a custom 200-case business evaluation suite**, spanning Marketing, Finance, Strategy, M&A and other supplied areas (F-D14/F-D15). A standard benchmark is not a launch prerequisite. Register original case versions, reviewed enhancements, lab requirement profile, suite membership, exposure history, qualification and protocol. Professor review of source cases does not establish calibrated automated grading, an official Harvard/Wharton benchmark or a comparable MBA-human baseline.

Report partner-supplied prior experiments as Partner-reported until reproduced. Case availability, source rights, substantive expert validation and current-model results are different evidence fields. Customer-specific case subsets/variants or changed scoring receive separate versions and comparison limitations; never blend them into an unchanged base-suite score.

Separate fields for official-comparable protocol results, adapted subsets and ADI custom/synthetic suites. Similar tasks or capability labels do not establish benchmark transfer. No training/export of protected benchmark questions/answers or resale as proprietary ADI tasks.

Record benchmark exposure, refresh status and known contamination limitations. Never silently change a suite behind an old score. Publication to a leaderboard is a separate explicitly authorized action, not part of normal reporting.

## 3. Evidence model and structure

ReportVersion pins assessment/protocol/pack versions and artifact digests, evidence cutoff, source labels, author, intended audience, limitations, reviewer decisions, disclosure permission, export references and correction history.

ClaimRecord contains exact wording, source metric/evidence, target/configuration, comparator, population/denominator, uncertainty, allowed audience and review status. Source origins: imported/published, ADI-executed, customer-reported, partner-reported or illustrative. Product readiness is independent: Specified, Prototype, Implemented, Integration-tested, Customer-accepted.

Required sections: question/scope; package coverage; protocol; results with counts/errors/exclusions; evidence-linked failure analysis; intervention or “Not performed”; independent generalization/transfer; uncertainty/negative results; permitted conclusion; evidence appendix. Unknown cost/version/sample data remain unknown.

AI can draft a narrative from evidence. It cannot invent results, change scientific verdicts, hide regressions, assign approvals or turn plausible explanations into causal findings. Quantitative performance claims must resolve to source metrics and relevant counts/units.

## 4. Review and export

Draft → Evidence checked → Technical review → Domain review where applicable → Audience approval → Approved → Exported. Changed/withdrawn evidence marks dependent reports Review required. Edits create a new immutable version. Blocked, Changes requested, Superseded and Withdrawn retain audit history.

Reviewer assignment/independence and disclosure authority remain open. Sales can request wording changes but cannot alter metrics, tests, uncertainty or scientific conclusions. Customer identities/private results need applicable disclosure permission; evidence possession is not permission to market it.

Preview may be an internal watermarked draft; external exports need completed applicable review and redaction. Proposed formats: PDF/HTML plus machine-readable metrics/evidence manifest; first formats remain to select. Export starts no evaluation, training, email, Notion publication or public leaderboard submission.

## 5. Sales catalogue and claim discipline

| Offer | Primary user / sponsor | Promise to scope | Not promised |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Diagnostic Pack | Evaluation researcher / capability lead | Compatible cases/runner, baseline and usable failure evidence | Training or guaranteed gain |
| Business Case Diagnostics 200 — first SKU, working name | Lab evaluation/capability lead; agent researcher | Qualified/enhanced business-case suite, agreed assessment, evidence-linked failure dossiers and proposed next experiments | Automatic rights to redistribute cases, guaranteed failure/uplift, professor validation of unreviewed derivatives or official school endorsement |
| Training Environment & Data Pack | RL/post-training engineer / team lead | Qualified usable assets/interfaces and permitted rights | ADI optimizer, static logs as an RL Gym, every backend supported |
| Controlled Improvement Study | Research scientist / research lead | Interpretable comparison of supplied baseline/intervention with independent tests | ADI-run weight optimization or a positive outcome |

First-call questions: Which capability? Which benchmark/internal evaluation? What fails today? What can your own synthetic pipeline already do? Do you need data, environment, diagnosis or a study? Which interface and data boundary? Who accepts the pilot and controls budget?

Demo to build: one capability/scenario → visible/hidden-state boundary → authorized reference/customer-policy run → success/failure/recovery inspection → graded failure hypothesis → targeted asset → controlled retest report. Use prominent illustrative labels until measured; do not fake live runs. Bring a manifest, grader fixtures, permitted traces, integration guide, rights statement and pilot acceptance sheet.

Say “proposed” until available. Say “customer-reported” unless reproduced. Say “system-level change” if model and harness both changed. Say “improvement on suite/version X” only with evidence; never replace it with “GDPval uplift” without the separate assessment. No generic “best agent,” sovereign-compliance or Domain AGI claim.

Contract acceptance concerns agreed validity/integration/evidence, not guaranteed research success. Existing historical prices and task counts are not approved prices/quotas for this product; pricing remains separate.

The 200-case collection scope is now user-confirmed; received, permitted, qualified and evaluated counts remain to establish. Do not copy historical task-unit pricing into this SKU without costing licenses, enhancement, faculty/expert review, harness integration, evaluation and adjudication. Detailed deliverables and the partial-input workflow are in [SKU 01](13-BUSINESS-CASE-DIAGNOSTICS-SKU.md).

## 6. Acceptance criteria

- F-RP01: No intervention yields a diagnostic report labeled Improvement not tested.
- F-RP02: ADI custom results cannot populate official-comparable benchmark fields.
- F-RP03: Unsupported/withdrawn evidence or missing audience permission blocks approved external export.
- F-RP04: Metrics reconcile to source counts/exclusions, including negative and incomplete outcomes.
- F-RP05: Customer-reported, illustrative and ADI-executed evidence remain distinct.
- F-RP06: Model+harness changes cannot produce a training-only claim.
- F-RP07: Redaction excludes hidden solutions, secrets, private traces and contributor identity material.
- F-RP08: Corrections retain prior exports and require review of affected claims.
- F-RP09: No report operation starts a model call or external publication automatically.
- F-RP10: Product readiness and measured scientific evidence have separate labels.

These are specifications only; no scientific or commercial performance result is supplied.
