# Financing decision

A fictional business requires at least USD 500,000 of funding and has USD 240,000 annual cash available for debt service. Choose one supplied offer satisfying minimum funding and a debt-service coverage ratio (DSCR) of at least 1.5. Among feasible offers minimize first-year cash debt service. Do not combine offers or invent refinancing.

Use the case's stipulated simplification: first-year interest = initial principal × annual rate; debt service = that interest + scheduled first-year principal repayment; DSCR = cash available / debt service. Ignore fees/tax/timing effects because they are not supplied. This is a synthetic exercise, not financial advice.

Return JSON with per-offer interest, debt service, DSCR and funding/coverage feasibility, plus `selected_option`, `constraint_checks`, `evidence` and `limitations`. Cite exhibits.json. Explain only the final rationale; do not provide private chain of thought.
